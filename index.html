A few helpful things

Before you get in touch.

How do I enquire about flowers?

Call 09 623 2104 or email the shop with your occasion, date and preferences. The website does not take payments or confirm orders.

Can I discuss wedding garlands?

Yes. Kensington advertises Indian wedding garlands and related bridal flowers. Share your date, colours and quantities when you make a wedding enquiry. Please confirm the available options directly.

What about delivery or a time-sensitive order?

Please call to check what is possible for your date and location. This website does not promise delivery, same-day service or a specific turnaround.
