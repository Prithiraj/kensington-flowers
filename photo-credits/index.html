Business information

Address, telephone and public email are supported by the Dominion Road directory. Services are based on the business’s indexed social posts. Opening-hour listings conflict, so no schedule or “open now” status is published. Prices, delivery policies and ratings have not been invented.

Fraunces and Source Sans 3 are served through Google Fonts. Both are available under the SIL Open Font License. The interface uses local serif and sans-serif fallbacks if fonts cannot load.
