## Excluded from published assets

Business Facebook/Instagram photographs, Google-review photographs, Maps/Street View screenshots and third-party storefront images with unverified reuse rights. These were research references only and are not downloaded by this build. The actual business portfolio remains accessible through direct social links.

## Replacing images

Obtain high-resolution owner-approved original photographs and record the photographer/rightsholder, written commercial-use permission, credit requirements, date and any relevant consent for recognisable people. Update each entry in `assets/photos.json`, its alt text and captions in the templates, and this register. Confirm that every photo accurately represents current business work before removing illustrative labels.

Do not use AI-generated flowers, interiors or people as proof of this business's work. A demo label is not a licence for copying an unapproved photo.
