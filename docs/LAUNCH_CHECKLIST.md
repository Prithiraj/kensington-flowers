## Before an owner-approved commercial launch

- [ ] Confirm the customer-facing name and relationship to Kensington Dairy & Flowers.
- [ ] Confirm 708 Dominion Road and the customer entrance; resolve the 706 discrepancy.
- [ ] Confirm phone and monitored email inbox.
- [ ] Confirm shop opening hours and whether floral ordering has different availability.
- [ ] Confirm current advertised services and preferred ceremony terminology.
- [ ] Obtain original business photos, written reuse clearance, required credits and relevant people consent.
- [ ] Replace illustrative images; update alt text, captions, licence register and social image.
- [ ] Obtain a current real exterior photograph; do not substitute a stock storefront.
- [ ] Verify any proposed reviews/testimonials and reuse requirements before adding them.
- [ ] Obtain explicit owner approval for public copy, facts, photos and privacy information.
- [ ] Perform manual keyboard, zoom, focus-obstruction and screen-reader acceptance testing on real devices.
- [ ] Review repeatable mobile lab performance; do not claim field Core Web Vitals without data.
- [ ] Validate final canonical URLs, schema and preview/social metadata for the production domain.
- [ ] Only then set `preview: false` and enable indexing; submit the sitemap through the owner's search tooling if approved.

## Optional later work — not part of this static preview

A real enquiry backend needs an approved recipient, provider, privacy terms, validation, spam controls, rate limits, failure handling and end-to-end delivery testing. Checkout requires owner-approved products, pricing, taxes, payments and fulfilment policies. Analytics requires a deliberate data/privacy decision. None is simulated here.
